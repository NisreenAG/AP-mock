13. [30 marks]

Tirzepatide (commercially known as Mounjaro) is a peptide-based medication used to treat type 2 diabetes. Tirzepatide binds to GIP receptors and GLP-1 receptors, which are G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) located in the plasma membrane of pancreatic β cells. Activation of either receptor can stimulate adenylyl cyclase, resulting in increased intracellular cyclic AMP (cAMP). Increased cAMP activates downstream proteins that contribute to insulin secretion. Researchers investigated the effect of tirzepatide on cultured pancreatic β cells. Equal numbers of genetically identical β cells were placed into four treatment groups. All groups were maintained at the same glucose concentration for 30 minutes. The researchers then measured intracellular cAMP concentration and the amount of insulin released into the culture medium. The data are shown in Table 1 below. 6 marksa. Describe the relationship between tirzepatide concentration and intracellular cAMP concentration shown in Table 1.
MARKING GUIDE
Increasing tirzepatide concentration increases intracellular cAMP. cAMP increases from 2.1 pmol/10⁶ cells in the control to 9.1 pmol/10⁶ cells at 100 nM tirzepatide (4 marks). However, the increase becomes smaller between 10 and 100 nM, suggesting that the response approaches a maximum (2 marks).
6 marksb. Calculate the percent increase in insulin secretion in cells treated with 10 nM tirzepatide compared with the control cells. Show your work.
MARKING GUIDE
[(9.7 − 3.2)/3.2] × 100 = 203.1% (accept a value ± 3.0) (2 marks for the work and 4 marks for the answer). Treatment with 10 nM tirzepatide resulted in approximately a 203% increase in insulin secretion compared with the control.
6 marksc. Based on the data, make a claim about the relationship between intracellular cAMP concentration and insulin secretion. Support your claim using evidence from Table 1.
MARKING GUIDE
• The data support the claim that increased intracellular cAMP is associated with increased insulin secretion (3 marks).
• For example, cAMP increased from 2.1 to 8.2 pmol/10⁶ cells between the control and 10 nM treatment, while insulin secretion increased from 3.2 to 9.7 ng/mL. Therefore, activation of the signaling pathway that increases cAMP is associated with increased insulin release (3 marks).
6 marksd. A researcher repeats the experiment using β cells containing a mutation that prevents activation of adenylyl cyclase. Predict the cAMP concentration and insulin secretion of the mutant cells after treatment with 100 nM tirzepatide compared with normal cells treated with 100 nM tirzepatide. Justify your prediction in terms of signal transduction.
MARKING GUIDE
• The mutant cells would have lower cAMP concentrations and reduced insulin secretion compared with normal cells treated with 100 nM tirzepatide (3 marks).
• Tirzepatide may still bind to its membrane receptors, but if adenylyl cyclase cannot be activated, the receptor signal cannot produce the normal increase in cAMP. Consequently, downstream cAMP-dependent signaling will be reduced, resulting in less stimulation of insulin secretion (3 marks).
6 markse. Patients with type 2 diabetes generally retain at least some functional pancreatic β cells, whereas type 1 diabetes results from autoimmune destruction of β cells. Explain why activation of GIP and GLP-1 receptors by tirzepatide can improve glucose regulation in many patients with type 2 diabetes but cannot replace insulin therapy in patients with type 1 diabetes.
MARKING GUIDE
In type 2 diabetes, functional β cells remain capable of producing insulin. Tirzepatide can therefore activate receptors on those β cells and enhance glucose-dependent insulin secretion (3 marks). In type 1 diabetes, autoimmune destruction greatly reduces the number of functional β cells. Consequently, activating GIP and GLP-1 receptors cannot generate sufficient endogenous insulin when the insulin-producing cells themselves are absent. Exogenous insulin is therefore still required (3 marks).

4 marksb. Based on the function of DNA polymerase and the data in Table 2, explain why increasing concentrations of Compound X result in the observed change in the amount of newly synthesized DNA.
MARKING GUIDE
• DNA polymerase catalyzes the addition of nucleotides to the 3′ end of a growing DNA strand during DNA replication (2 marks).
• Because Compound X reduces DNA polymerase activity, increasing concentrations of Compound X decrease the rate at which nucleotides are added to newly synthesized DNA strands (2 marks).
7 marksc. Using the DNA sequences in Table 3, determine the sequence and directionality of the mRNA transcribed from this region of GENE1. Explain how complementary base pairing and strand directionality result in the mRNA sequence.
MARKING GUIDE
• The DNA template strand is 3′–TAC CCG AAA GGT ACT–5′, therefore the mRNA sequence is 5′–AUG GGC UUU CCA UGA–3′ (5 marks; 1 for each codon. Do not award partial marks if one base is incorrect in any given codon).
• During transcription, RNA polymerase uses the DNA template strand to synthesize a complementary RNA molecule. RNA polymerase reads the DNA template in the 3′ → 5′ direction while synthesizing the RNA in the 5′ → 3′ direction (1 mark).
• Complementary base pairing determines the RNA sequence, with A pairing with U, T with A, C with G, and G with C (1 mark).
9 marksd. Using your answer from part (c) and the genetic code chart in Table 4, determine the amino acid sequence produced during translation. Explain how interactions between mRNA, tRNA, and the ribosome result in the specified amino acid sequence.
MARKING GUIDE
• The amino acid sequence is Met–Gly–Phe–Pro (4 marks; 1 for each codon. If the student adds the term STOP to the end of the sequence, deduct 1 mark).
• During translation, the ribosome moves along the mRNA and reads its codons (1 mark). tRNA molecules with complementary anticodons bind to the appropriate mRNA codons (1 mark), and each tRNA carries the amino acid corresponding to that codon (1 mark). The ribosome catalyzes peptide-bond formation between the amino acids (1 mark), producing the polypeptide in the order specified by the mRNA. When the ribosome reaches the UGA stop codon, translation terminates (1 mark).
